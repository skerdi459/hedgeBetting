Enter the odds from Site A (the one that refunds part of a losing stake) and from Site B or an exchange. The slip tells you whether to play the match and exactly what to bet where — then track each match and keep a running balance per site.

The match

Site A pays 8% of turnover (set in Balance → Edit starting balances) + 20% of net loss. Change the rates there.

Two-way: fill the Site A odd for the side you back and its Cover (DC) double chance — back 1→cover X2, back X→cover 12, back 2→cover 1X. Three-way: fill all three Site B odds. Decimal odds. Commas work too (3,26). Exchange lay is optional.

You get the 28% refund only when this ticket loses. If this result actually comes in, there is no commission — that outcome is your loss.

Saving adds the match to the tracker as pending with the option shown in the slip. When the result is known, tap the winner to settle it into your balance.

Tracked matches

    Keep in mind: The 28% refund is not applied at settlement — matches settle on the real cash that moves. The refund is calculated monthly as 28% of your net Site A loss (losing stakes minus any winning-pick payouts), so a winning backed pick reduces it and a net-positive month earns nothing. See “Monthly refunds” in the dashboard; when a month's payout lands, press “Mark paid → Site A”. If the refund is a bonus/free bet rather than cash, lower the rate. Odds move between placing bets, so confirm each before the next. Bookmakers may limit accounts that arbitrage. Figures use rounded stakes, so results can differ by a few cents.
